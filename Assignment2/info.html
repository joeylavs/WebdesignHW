About Outlast

Outlast is a psychological survival horror game developed by Red Barrels. It was released in 2013 and quickly became known for its intense atmosphere, terrifying enemies, and found-footage style presentation. The game takes place inside Mount Massive Asylum, a remote psychiatric hospital with a dark history and even darker secrets.

The story follows Miles Upshur, a freelance journalist who receives a tip about strange experiments happening inside the asylum. When he arrives, he finds the place overrun by violent patients, failed experiments, and something much worse lurking deeper inside. The game mixes mystery, horror, and tension as you uncover what the Murkoff Corporation has been doing behind closed doors.

Outlast is widely praised for its sound design, lighting, and the way it builds fear without relying on cheap jumpscares. It’s one of the games that helped define modern indie horror, and it still holds up today as one of the scariest experiences you can play.
