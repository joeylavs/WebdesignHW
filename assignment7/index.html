THE FUTURE OF WORK

Technology should support people, not replace what makes us human.

WHO WE ARE

People belong at the center of progress.

Human First is an advocacy organization focused on helping workers and businesses navigate a changing workplace.

We believe technology can be useful without losing the creativity, judgment, relationships, and ideas that people bring to their work.

“AI can change how we work without changing the value of being human.”

WHAT MATTERS

Protecting the human side of work.

Our work focuses on the qualities technology should support, rather than replace.

01

Creativity

Supporting original thinking, imagination, and the ability to approach problems in new ways.

02

Keeping people involved when decisions require context, responsibility, and careful judgment.

03

Relationships

Protecting meaningful collaboration, communication, trust, and human connection.

Responsible AI

Encouraging thoughtful boundaries for how AI is used throughout the workplace.
