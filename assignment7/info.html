Change should support people.

Artificial intelligence is changing how people work across many industries. Understanding these changes can help workers and organizations make better decisions about where AI belongs.

Human First supports an approach where technology assists people while human creativity, judgment, relationships, accountability, and safety remain important parts of the workplace.
