Outlast Information

What is Outlast?

Outlast is a survival horror game developed by Red Barrels. It focuses on stealth, fear, and psychological tension instead of combat.

You explore Mount Massive Asylum, uncovering disturbing experiments and trying to survive the horrors inside.

Story Overview

The game follows Miles Upshur, a journalist who receives a mysterious tip about unethical experiments happening at Mount Massive Asylum.

When he arrives, he discovers the inmates have taken over, and something far worse is lurking in the dark.

Full Story: Outlast (2013)

Outlast begins with Miles sneaking into Mount Massive Asylum to investigate the Murkoff Corporation. Inside, he discovers the inmates have been transformed by experiments involving a creature called the Walrider, a nanotech entity created through psychological manipulation.

As Miles explores, he uncovers documents revealing that Murkoff tortured patients to create “hosts” for the Walrider. Miles eventually becomes the new host, gaining supernatural abilities but losing control of his fate.

The game ends with Miles being hunted by Murkoff soldiers — but the Walrider inside him survives.

Full Story: Outlast 2 (2017)

Outlast 2 follows Blake Langermann, a cameraman investigating the murder of a pregnant woman. Blake and his wife crash in the Arizona desert, where they encounter two cults obsessed with the end of the world.

Throughout the game, Blake experiences hallucinations of his childhood trauma — suggesting that Murkoff is once again using mind‑control experiments. Strange signals in the desert cause visions, memory loss, and violent behavior.

The story ends ambiguously, with Blake witnessing impossible events that imply Murkoff’s influence is still active.

How Outlast 1 & 2 Are Connected

While Outlast 1 and 2 feature different characters and locations, they are connected through the Murkoff Corporation.

  • • Murkoff continues its experiments after the Walrider incident.
  • • Outlast 2’s hallucinations are caused by Murkoff’s radio‑wave mind control tests.
  • • Documents in Outlast 2 reference Mount Massive and the Walrider program.
  • • Both games show Murkoff expanding its unethical research across the country.

Outlast 2 isn’t a direct sequel — it’s a parallel story showing Murkoff’s experiments spreading beyond the asylum.

Key Features

  • • First-person horror experience
  • • No weapons — pure survival
  • • Night vision camera mechanic
  • • Intense chase sequences
  • • Disturbing atmosphere and sound design
