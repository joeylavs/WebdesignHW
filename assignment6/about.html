Outlast Gameplay Overview

A closer look at how Outlast builds fear through mechanics, pacing, and atmosphere.

How Outlast Plays

Outlast is a first-person survival horror game where you play as Miles Upshur, a journalist who sneaks into Mount Massive Asylum to investigate what’s going on inside. The gameplay is all about staying alive without being able to fight back. You don’t get weapons, so you’re constantly running, hiding, and trying not to get caught.

The game uses darkness as one of its biggest mechanics. You rely on a camcorder’s night vision to see, but the battery drains fast, so you’re always searching for more batteries while trying not to panic. Most of the tension comes from hearing enemies before you see them, or catching a glimpse of something moving in the shadows.

Outlast’s gameplay is simple but intense. You open doors slowly, squeeze through tight spaces, hide under beds or inside lockers, and try to outsmart enemies who patrol the asylum. Every chase feels chaotic, and the game does a great job of making you feel powerless in a place where everything wants you dead.
