Outlast Information

What is Outlast?

Outlast is a survival horror game developed by Red Barrels. It focuses on stealth, fear, and psychological tension instead of combat. You explore Mount Massive Asylum, uncovering disturbing experiments and trying to survive the horrors inside.

Story Overview

The game follows Miles Upshur, a journalist who receives a mysterious tip about unethical experiments happening at Mount Massive Asylum. When he arrives, he discovers the inmates have taken over, and something far worse is lurking in the dark.

Key Features

  • • First-person horror experience
  • • No weapons — pure survival
  • • Night vision camera mechanic
  • • Intense chase sequences
  • • Disturbing atmosphere and sound design
