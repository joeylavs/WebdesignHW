**Visual Appeal**

**Which icons did you ultimately use and for what purposes?** 

I used horror themed icon like a ghost and skull to help with the horror theme. They help guide the user through each section and helps the vibe of the page. 

**Which consistent colors, values, lines, and/or shapes did you utilize and why?**

I used dark backgrounds and red accents to help the horror aesthetic. The dark background helps the red accents make everything pop which is why I chose it

**Which Google Fonts did you use and for what purposes?**  

I used one bold stylized font for headings to give the site personality and a cleaner font for the main text so everything stays readable. 

**Structure & Organization**

**What information does your table organize, and why was a table appropriate?**

My table organizes horror game info that would look messy in paragraph form. A table just makes it easier to scan and compare things quickly.

**What specific style choices helped you structure and organize your form?**  

I centered the form spaced out the inputs, and kept the labels simple. It makes the form easy to follow even with the darker theme going on.

**Design Goals**

**What is the goal of your form, and how did it support your site's goals?**

The form gives users a way to interact with the site like sending feedback or connecting. It supports the site by making it feel more like an actual project instead of just a static page.

**How does the gallery support your website's goals?**  

The gallery shows off the horror imagery and icons which helps set the mood and makes the page more interesting to scroll through.

**What pattern did you use: uniform, alternating, or something else, and why?**  

I went with a uniform pattern so everything lines up nicely. It keeps the gallery clean and easy to look at.
